# Commercial license

Accuretta is free software under the [GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). This page explains when that's
all you need, and when you need a commercial license instead.

## Free under the AGPL

You can use Accuretta for free, also at work and inside a company, as long as you follow the
AGPL. In short:

- You can run it, study it and change it.
- If you **give** a modified version to others, you give them its source code too, under the
  AGPL.
- If people **use** a modified version over a network (for example, colleagues opening its
  web interface), you offer them its source code too.

Running an unmodified copy for yourself or your team needs nothing extra.

## When you need a commercial license

Ask for a commercial license if any of these is true:

- You want to build Accuretta, or a modified version of it, into a product you sell or ship
  without publishing your source code.
- You want to offer Accuretta, or a modified version, as a hosted service without publishing
  your changes.
- Your company's policy doesn't allow AGPL software.

A commercial license lets you do these things without the AGPL's source-sharing duties.

## How to ask

Contact the maintainer through the project homepage, <https://www.sekura.se/accuretta>, or
open an issue titled "Commercial license" at
<https://github.com/mkultraware/accuretta/issues>. Say who you are, what you want to do with
Accuretta, and roughly how many people will use it.
